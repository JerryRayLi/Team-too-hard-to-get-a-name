# Playtest Intent

The INITIATION levels teach the core mechanics while establishing the game's trial-and-error difficulty. They are not intended to be failure-free.

During playtests, distinguish between these outcomes:

- The player does not understand the rule or why they died.
- The player understands the rule but misses the execution or timing.
- The player ignores an available hint and repeats the same strategy.
- The player adapts after observing the level.

Record whether a hint changes the player's behavior, rather than treating death count alone as evidence that a level is too difficult.
